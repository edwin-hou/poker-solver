Transparent by design

What the all-street solver computes

Poker Solver uses the configure → solve → inspect workflow familiar from modern GTO study tools while keeping its smaller browser-scale abstractions explicit.

01

Choose the starting street

Build a heads-up preflop push/fold game or start directly on a flop, turn, or river board with weighted two-card ranges.

02

Expand exact combos

The 169 hand classes become suit-specific holdings. Public cards and the opponent's hand remove impossible combinations.

03

Train with CFR+

Preflop, flop, and turn sample future boards. River retains exact showdown values and exact finite-tree best responses.

04

Inspect the solution

Browse decision nodes, action-colored range matrices, combo frequencies, EV, and exact or Monte Carlo exploitability.

Important scope

All streets are available, but this is not one commercial-scale game tree

Preflop is a heads-up push/fold abstraction. Flop and turn solve only the selected betting street and sample later cards without later-street betting. River solves its complete configured check/bet/fold/call tree exactly. Postflop raises, multiway play, rake, ICM, four-bets, and a monolithic preflop-to-river no-limit tree remain future work.
