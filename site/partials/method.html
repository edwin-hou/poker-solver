Transparent by design

What this solver actually computes

RiverForge borrows the workflow of commercial poker study tools while keeping the game definition, strategy, and convergence metric inspectable.

01

Expand exact combos

Standard 169-class notation becomes individual two-card holdings. The five-card board removes blocked combinations before any training begins.

02

Evaluate real Hold'em hands

Every remaining combo receives an exact seven-card score. Showdown payoffs therefore use actual pairs, straights, flushes, boats, and card removal.

03

Minimize counterfactual regret

Chance-sampled CFR+ repeatedly deals compatible holdings, updates action regrets, and forms a linearly weighted average strategy.

04

Attack the strategy

An exact evaluator computes each player's best information-consistent response. NashConv divided by two is reported as exploitability.

Scope and roadmap

Genuine Hold'em, intentionally bounded tree

The hosted edition solves one heads-up river betting street with multiple bet sizes and fold/call responses. It does not reproduce GTO Wizard's proprietary database, multi-street abstractions, or server-scale compute. Raising, turn/flop chance nodes, range locking, and subgame decomposition are natural next layers in the open-source engine.
